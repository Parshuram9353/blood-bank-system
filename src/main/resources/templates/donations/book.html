Health Guidelines
  • Get a good night's sleep before your donation.
  • Eat a healthy meal before your donation. Avoid fatty foods.
  • Drink plenty of water (500ml) starting the day before.
  • Bring a valid photo ID (Driver's License, Aadhaar, Passport).
  • You must not have donated blood in the last 90 days.
